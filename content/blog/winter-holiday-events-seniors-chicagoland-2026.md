# Holiday Lights, Markets, and Flower Shows: Chicagoland's Best Winter Events for Seniors (2026)

*November 1, 2026 · 7 min read · Active Chicagoland*

![An older couple walking arm in arm beneath strings of glowing holiday lights](/blog/winter-holiday-events-seniors-chicagoland-2026-hero.png)

Chicago does winter well. The cold is real, but so is the magic: a German Christmas market in the middle of the Loop, millions of lights glowing through the zoo, tropical greenhouses full of poinsettias, and a 30th birthday for one of the city's favorite traditions.

Plenty of it is **free**, a lot of it is **easy on the feet**, and most of it runs from mid-November into early January. Here's your guide to the season.

## Free Holiday Classics

### Christkindlmarket: 30 Years of Mulled Wine and Ornaments

The Christkindlmarket turns **30** this year and opens **Friday, November 20, 2026.** Admission is **free** at all three locations. Bring cash -- many vendors don't take cards.

- **Daley Plaza (Loop)**, 50 W. Washington St. Daily **Nov. 20 – Dec. 24.** Sun–Thu 11 a.m.–8 p.m., Fri–Sat 11 a.m.–9 p.m.
- **Wrigleyville**, Gallagher Way, 3635 N. Clark St. Daily **Nov. 20 – Dec. 31** (closed Christmas Day). Mon–Thu 3–9 p.m., Fri–Sat 11 a.m.–9 p.m., Sun 11 a.m.–7 p.m.
- **Aurora**, RiverEdge Park, 360 N. Broadway. The largest market, with **60+ vendors.** Open **Thursday–Sunday, Nov. 20 – Dec. 24.**

All three close at 4 p.m. on Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve.

**Senior tip:** go on a **weekday afternoon** -- evenings and weekends get packed. If you live in the western suburbs, the Aurora market is easier to reach and park at.

![Two older women in knit hats laughing as they clink mugs at a holiday market stall](/blog/winter-holiday-events-seniors-chicagoland-2026-market.png)

### Chicago's Christmas Tree Lighting

The official city tree lights up **Friday, November 20, 2026**, in **Millennium Park** near Michigan Avenue and Washington Street. It's free, outdoors, and the same day the Christkindlmarket opens a block away.

**Senior tip:** the crowd is huge. If you'd rather skip the crush, visit the tree on a weekday evening the following week -- it looks just as beautiful.

![A grandmother and grandchild gazing up at a giant lit Christmas tree downtown](/blog/winter-holiday-events-seniors-chicagoland-2026-tree.png)

### ZooLights: Free on Mondays

Lincoln Park Zoo's **ZooLights** runs nightly from **November 21, 2026, to January 3, 2027**, starting at 4:30 p.m. Tickets run **$7 to $20** depending on the date -- but **Mondays are free with registration** (except December 21 and 28).

![An older couple smiling in front of glowing giraffe and penguin light sculptures on a snowy zoo path](/blog/winter-holiday-events-seniors-chicagoland-2026-zoo.png)

## Flower Shows: Warm, Green, and Mostly Free

When it's 15 degrees outside, nothing beats a warm greenhouse full of poinsettias.

### Garfield Park Conservatory

The **2026–2027 Winter Flower Show** runs **November 25, 2026 – January 3, 2027**, and it's **included with general admission**:

- **Free for Chicago residents** (bring ID)
- **$5 for seniors 65+** who live outside the city
- **Closed Mondays and Tuesdays**; reservations recommended
- **Free parking**, plus a limited number of free wheelchairs

300 N. Central Park Ave., Chicago

### Lincoln Park Conservatory

The **Lincoln Park Conservatory** is **free**, with spots reserved in advance. Last season's winter show ran Wednesday–Sunday, 10 a.m.–3 p.m. -- check the Chicago Park District for this year's dates.

2391 N. Stockton Dr., Chicago

![An older woman in a red coat smiling as she admires red and white poinsettias in a glass conservatory](/blog/winter-holiday-events-seniors-chicagoland-2026-poinsettias.png)

## The Big Light Shows (Worth the Ticket)

These cost money, but they're the showstoppers of the season. Each one is a real walk, so wear your warmest boots.

### Illumination at The Morton Arboretum (Lisle)

- **November 14, 2026 – January 2, 2027**
- About a **one-mile walking trail**, described as **ADA-accessible**
- **22 light displays**, including four new large-scale installations and a 400-foot aurora-inspired canopy
- An indoor **model train** walk-through with nearly 1,000 feet of track ($9 add-on for nonmembers)
- Adult tickets **start at $22** (prices vary by date)
- A quieter **Sensory Night on November 17**, with static lights, softer music, and limited capacity

### Holiday Magic at Brookfield Zoo Chicago

- **November 19, 2026 – January 3, 2027**, 3–9 p.m.
- **More than 3.5 million lights**, with light tunnels, a maze, and animal-themed displays
- Tickets **from $29.95**; zoo members get in free

### Lightscape at Chicago Botanic Garden (Glencoe)

- **November 13, 2026 – January 3, 2027**, on select dates
- A **1.3-mile** illuminated trail
- Buy early -- popular dates sell out

**Senior tip for all three:** book an **early time slot on a weeknight.** Smaller crowds, slightly warmer, and you're home before 8.

![Four smiling older friends taking a selfie inside a glowing tunnel of holiday lights](/blog/winter-holiday-events-seniors-chicagoland-2026-tunnel.png)

## Christmas Around the World (Hyde Park)

The **Griffin Museum of Science and Industry's** **Christmas Around the World** exhibit features **more than 50 trees**, each decorated by community volunteers to represent a different culture -- a tradition more than 80 years old. Last season ran from early November to early January; check the museum's website for 2026 dates and ticket prices.

**Senior tip:** it's all indoors, with seating nearby -- an easy outing on a bad-weather day.

## Tips for Enjoying the Season Comfortably

- **Go early or on weekdays.** Smaller crowds, easier parking, better views.
- **Dress in layers** and wear **waterproof boots with good grip.** Outdoor paths can be icy.
- **Bring a folding cane seat** for the long outdoor light trails.
- **Take transit downtown.** Parking near Daley Plaza and Millennium Park is tough in December.
- **Carry cash** for the Christkindlmarket.
- **Check accessibility before you buy.** Most light trails are paved, but ask about wheelchairs and seating.
- **Go with a group.** Many senior centers and park districts run holiday bus trips to lights and markets -- check our directory for trips near you.

## The Bottom Line

You don't need a big budget to celebrate in Chicagoland. Between free markets, free flower shows, free Monday ZooLights, and the tree in Millennium Park, you could fill the whole season without buying a ticket. And if you splurge, make it a light show on a quiet weeknight with someone you love.
