## New listing

### Wauconda Township — Senior Programs
- Source: https://www.waucondatownship.com/seniorprograms (also mirrored at /senior-resources)
- Fetched directly — both pages confirmed content verbatim (no block encountered, unlike most municipal sites this project has hit).
- Free weekly Cards and Game Players Club (Thu 12:30-3:30pm — pinochle, canasta, golf, dominoes, sequence) and Zumba Gold ($5/class, Mon 10-11am), both at Township Hall, 505 W. Bonner Road.
- Genuinely distinct government body and physical venue from the Park District (Community Center) — no overlap.
- Neither activity is explicitly age-gated on the page itself, but both sit under the Township's senior-services umbrella (SHIP counseling, medical-equipment lending, senior transportation) alongside age-60+ services — flagged via qualityNote rather than treated as confirmed age-restricted.
- Contact name inconsistent across the Township's own pages (Lisa Knight vs. Peggy Gillen) — not included in the listing to avoid publishing a possibly-stale name.

## Considered, not included

### Wauconda Area Public Library
- Fetched directly (homepage + events calendar). No recurring senior/50+/55+ program category — only general adult programs (book clubs, crafts). The only senior-labeled items ("Fun in the Sun Senior Picnic," "Jack Frost New Year's Senior Brunch") are annual one-off events co-sponsored with the Park District/Township. Excluded per the no-one-off-events rule.

### Wauconda Township Historical Society (Andrew C. Cook House)
- Fetched directly. No stated admission fee or senior discount; open by appointment only, and its programming (Maypole Dance, Ice Cream & Pie Social, cemetery walk) is seasonal one-off events. Doesn't clear the bar.
