# Glenview wave — review checklist

Context: Glenview had 1 pre-existing listing (The East Wing Glenview Senior
Center) but no `/city/glenview` hub page. This wave adds 3 verified venues,
clearing the bar well past the minimum, and adds Glenview to `lib/cities.ts`
for the first time.

## New listings added (3)

### 1. Glenview Public Library — Older Adult Services
- **sourceUrl:** https://www.glenviewpl.org/outreach
- Homebound delivery (every 4 weeks), Tales & Travel Memory Kits for dementia caregivers, Next Chapter Book Club for adults with intellectual/developmental disabilities — all confirmed directly, with named staff contacts.
- **Verify:** nothing flagged.

### 2. The Grove National Historic Landmark
- **sourceUrl:** https://glenviewparks.org/facilities/the-grove/
- Free, 150-acre National Historic Landmark, weekend docent tours 11am-3pm — confirmed directly on the Park District's own page.
- **Note:** an initial search result described this as "124-acre"; the official page I fetched says 150 acres — used the directly-fetched figure.
- **Verify:** nothing else flagged.

### 3. Village of Glenview — Senior Services
- **sourceUrl:** https://www.glenview.il.us/625/Senior-Services
- Free medical equipment loan closet, Medicare/insurance guidance, support groups, Senior Housing Assistance Program — confirmed directly, including named coordinator and program eligibility figures (62+, income limits).
- **Verify:** nothing flagged.
